India Ka Sabse Popular Sports League Kaunsa Hai?

“IPL ko compare hi kyun karna hai kisi aur league se?”

Ye line mujhe Bengaluru ke ek sports café mein sunne ko mili thi. TV par Royal Challengers Bengaluru ka match chal raha tha. Virat Kohli batting kar rahe the aur crowd har boundary par aise react kar raha tha jaise World Cup final ho.

Table ke dusre side baitha ek football fan turant bola:

“Thoda wait karo. Football grow ho raha hai. Kabaddi bhi.”

Argument wahin shuru hua.

Aur sach kahun, ye debate India ke sports landscape ko samajhne ke liye kaafi interesting hai.

India ka sabse popular sports league kaunsa hai?

Surface level par jawab simple lagta hai.

IPL.

Lekin agar hum popularity ko sirf TV ratings ya social media followers tak limited na rakhein, toh discussion kaafi layered ho jata hai.

Kyunki popularity ka matlab sirf logon ka dekhna nahi hota.

Popularity ka matlab hota hai:

  • Log kitna emotionally invest hain
  • League kitni cultural conversations create karti hai
  • Young athletes ko kitna inspire karti hai
  • Cities aur communities ko kitna connect karti hai
  • Aur sabse important, kitne log apna waqt aur paisa us par kharch karte hain

Isi lens se dekhte hain Indian sports leagues ki duniya ko.
